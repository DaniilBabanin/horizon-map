Hover the map to check any spot (long-press on a touch screen): whether it is visible, and if not, how many metres it sits below your line of sight. Hover the panorama to find which ridge forms the skyline in that direction.

Terrain is AWS Terrain Tiles (Mapzen Terrarium: SRTM and other sources, about 100 m per pixel), worldwide between 85° S and 85° N. Earth curvature and refraction are included. Forest canopy and large buildings are partly in the data; individual houses and trees near you are not, so in a city your real view is narrower than shown.
