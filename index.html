Hover the map to check any spot (on a touch screen: tap to check, long-press to stand there): whether it is visible, and if not, how many metres it sits below your line of sight. Hover the panorama (or slide a finger along it) to find which ridge forms the skyline in that direction; scroll or pinch on it to zoom in, drag to turn, double-click to reset.

Terrain is AWS Terrain Tiles (Mapzen Terrarium: SRTM and other sources, about 100 m per pixel), worldwide between 85° S and 85° N. Earth curvature and refraction are included. Forest canopy and large buildings are partly in the data; individual houses and trees near you are not, so in a city your real view is narrower than shown.
